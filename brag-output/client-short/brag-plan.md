# Brag plan: Steplight, client cut

## What is this app?
Steplight records a business process once in a web app and publishes the training for it: Word, PDF and PowerPoint guides, Show me / Try it / Test me players, SCORM, a learning portal, and live in-app guidance ("Guide me"). When the app changes, Replay re-runs the recording to heal and refresh everything, in 7 languages.

## The angle
Training rots the day the software updates. The film shows exactly that happening: a guide's highlight keeps pointing at a button that has moved. Then it shows Steplight fixing it in the real product UI. The audience is prospects who own ERP or CRM rollouts. They don't need the mechanics, just the relief.

## Hook (0–4.5 s)
Frame 0 is a punch-in on the real Northwind ERP toolbar. Steplight's own teal highlight sits on **New**, under a guide callout reading "STEP 1 OF 10 · Click **New**." On "updated overnight" the app switches to its new layout. The highlight is left pointing at the wrong thing, turns red, and an "Out of date" chip lands.

## Key moments
- **Friction:** the real exported Word pages and PowerPoint slides. Each screenshot is marked out of date one by one, the step list gets rewritten, and the page flips to Danish. Then a cursor hunts around the changed app.
- **Record:** the cursor clicks **New** and types "Fjord Kontormøbler ApS". The recording pill counts steps, and the Studio step list fills with the real auto-written instructions: "Click **New**." and "In the **Name** field, enter **Fjord Kontormøbler ApS**."
- **One recording, every output:** a montage of real exports, each with its own entrance. The Word page, the PDF, the PowerPoint slide, the Show me player, Try it, the Test me score, then the Studio export menu with the light landing on "SCORM 1.2 package".
- **Guide me:** the real Assist callout walks steps 1 → 2 → 3 in the live app.
- **Replay heals (payoff):** the app changes layout, and Steplight's light follows **New** from the toolbar to the ribbon. The step list ticks ok and healed, the thumbnails refresh, and "Updated · 3 healed" lands. The 3 is from the real run.
- **Languages:** the language chip rolls through the 7 language names and lands on Dansk. "Click **New**." becomes "Klik på **Ny**." with the Danish screenshot.

## Outro
The light lands centre frame and becomes the Steplight mark: the staircase draws on and the dot lights up. The wordmark follows, then "Record once. Publish everywhere." and a **Book a demo** button, held for about 3 s.

## User flow worth showing
Record (click New, type the name) → steps appear → outputs → Guide me in the app → Replay after an update.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished` × `app-store`
- Creative direction: a quiet premium product film; the real UI does the talking.
- Interpretation: one hero move per shot, expo-out arrivals, held reads, no hype words, the accent rationed to the light and the CTA.

## Format: landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps
## Duration: 65.4 s (set by the narration; the user asked for about 60 s, overriding brag's 15–25 s)

## Share copy (draft)
Your software updated overnight. Your training didn't. Steplight records a process once, publishes the guides, practice, tests and in-app help, and re-runs itself when the app changes.

## Audio direction
- Role: a warm bed under a voice, with sparse accents that match the motion.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11` (114.8 BPM). The file starts at 2.78 s so its B section opens at film 14.07 s, as the product is named ("the music opens when the product appears"). The bed is low through the hook and friction.
- Treatment: a volume lane at about 0.10 under the hook, ducked to about 0.14 under the voice after the reveal, up to 0.32 when the voice ends, then a 1.5 s fade.
- Cue guidance: vol-11 strong cues every ~2.1 s. The reveal is locked to the section change; the logo lands near a strong beat.
- Audio-reactive: none. The guidance bans breathing and pulsing; the light only moves with a cause.
- SFX (sparse): real cursor clicks (`interface/click_003`, `ui/click2`), soft key ticks while typing, a soft drop on chips (`interface/drop_002`), one `impactSoft_medium` on the stale stamp, and `impactBell_heavy_000` on the mark.
- Restraint: no sound without a visible cause, no whoosh on every cut, and nothing louder than the voice.

## Voiceover script (af_heart, Kokoro, speed 0.94)
See `vo-script.json`. Measured timings are in `composition/assets/vo/timing.json`.
1. Hook: "Your software updated overnight. Your training didn't."
2. Friction: "Now someone has to re-shoot every screenshot, rewrite every step, and translate it all again. Until they do, your people are guessing."
3. Reveal: "Introducing Steplight. Do the process once, in your browser, and Steplight writes the guide for you."
4. Record: "Every click becomes a clear, written step, with the screenshot already captured, and highlighted."
5. Outputs: "From that one recording, you get Word, PDF and PowerPoint guides, video walkthroughs, hands-on practice, scored tests, and SCORM packages for your LMS."
6. Guide me: "Inside the app itself, Guide me walks people through it, live, one step at a time."
7. Replay: "When the software changes, press Replay. Steplight re-runs the process, finds what moved, and refreshes every screenshot."
8. Languages: "Every guide also comes in seven languages, without recording again."
9. End card: "Steplight. Record it once, and it stays right."
